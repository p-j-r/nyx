# Brillouin gain of the four ring chips, rebuilt in Rust

Backward scattering of the design mode with itself at 1550 nm; rib on slab on r-plane sapphire (c axis 57.6 degrees from the surface normal, tilted towards the propagation direction at azimuth psi = 0); wafer frame (x lateral, y propagation, z normal). Silicon c11 = 165.7 GPa, optical n_Si = 3.476, sapphire uniaxial n_o = 1.7462, n_e = 1.7383 with the tilted crystal tensor (exact coupled mode solver). Gains are in 1/(W m) with the mechanical Q capped at 1000, power and group velocity from the mode fields.

Columns: PE and MB are the photoelastic and moving-boundary gains alone (`|f_PE|`, `|f_MB|` in the gain formula); total combines both overlap integrals with their phase. *PE, as is* and *total, as is* use the strain without complex conjugate.
