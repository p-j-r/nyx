# 128. Structured curvilinear PMLs, outward absorbing layers, native mixed-cell FEM kernels, and 3-tier open boundaries

Date: 2026-11-25

## Status

Accepted. Amends ADR 4 (`SimplexMesh` as the sole solver discretisation) and ADR 69 (axisymmetric cylindrical stretch and PML workflow); fulfils ADR 5 and ADR 32 (native mixed-cell solver consumption without simplex splitting); extends ADR 52, ADR 54, ADR 61, ADR 82, ADR 101, ADR 106, ADR 115, ADR 120, and ADR 123.

## Context

Modeling open and unbounded domains across `nyx`'s core photonics, phononics, superconducting cQED, and static chip workloads exposed five structural and physical limitations:

1. **Simplex-only solver splitting (`ADR 4`, `Option A`):** Although `CellMesh<D>` (`ADR 5`, `ADR 32`) already represented `Quad` (2D) and `Prism` + `Hex` (3D) structured layers, `Mesh::discretise()` cut every `Quad` into $2$ triangles, every `Prism` into $3$ tetrahedra, and every `Hex` into $6$ chiral tetrahedra before `Physics::solve`. Splitting inflates PML cell counts by $2\times\text{–}6\times$ and PML DOFs by $29\%\text{–}57\%$, introduces oblique Nédélec diagonal edges that mix normal exponential decay with tangential phase, breaks $C_{4v}$ / rotational mirror symmetry of degenerate cavity doublets, and causes severe logarithmic tangential locking under singular $(1 - t)^{-2}$ poles—preventing true Rational PMLs and Rational Infinite Elements.
2. **Inward CAD slicing & missing curvilinear topologies (`ADR 52`, `ADR 69`):** Legacy `geom::Op::Pml` required users to manually pad every background box by `+thickness`, draw a dummy `window` primitive, and boolean-slice up to 26 inward CAD regions. `structured_pml` defaulted to `false` with backwards `pml_relax = 1.5` coarsening, `layer_grading` was conflated with polynomial stretch `order`, `nyx-ems::axisym` rejected meshes touching $r = 0$, `geom::stack` rejected standalone 3D `Sphere` hosts, and 2D/3D Cylindrical and Spherical structured layers (and ray-invariance checks preventing $\partial_R\varepsilon \neq 0$ across layered wafers) did not exist.
3. **Centroid AABB stretching & magic scaling:** Evaluating coordinate stretching once per simplex centroid against axis-aligned bounding boxes created an $\mathcal{O}(h_u)$ staircase impedance reflection floor, could not represent off-diagonal cylindrical/spherical metric pullbacks, and relied on a hardcoded `strength = 3.0` instead of wavelength-calibrated dimensionless scaling continuous across multi-material interfaces.
4. **Anisotropic elastic backward waves & sign masking (`nyx-sms`):** In rotated crystals (`Si [110]`, tilted R-plane `Sapphire`, `LiNbO3`) and $S_1$ Lamb membranes, modes with antiparallel phase and group velocity ($k_u v_{g,u} < 0$) are exponentially amplified by standard PMLs; `nyx-sms` lacked pre-solve Christoffel detection or M-PML/adiabatic stabilization, masked amplifying `f.im < 0` eigenvalues behind `f.im.abs()`, and left outer PML walls traction-free by default (spawning zero-frequency rigid-body modes).
5. **Incomplete static/diffusion open boundaries (`nyx-dfs`):** `far_field` Robin BCs (`ADR 120`) were wired only on `electrostatic`, leaving `heat_stationary`, `magnetostatic`, layered 3D dielectric chips, and `nyx-sms::static` without asymptotic Robin or volumetric Infinite Elements.

## Decision

* **Native mixed-cell solver topology (`Option B`, amending ADR 4, fulfilling ADR 5 & ADR 32; `femcore::mesh::mixed`, `femcore::{fem_mixed,edge_mixed}`, `nyx_physics::MixedToSimplexProjector`):**
  * `MixedDiscretisation` (`Mesh::discretise_mixed`) keeps `Tri` + `Quad` (2D) and `Tet` + `Prism` + `Hex` (3D) cells uncut from `MeshedGeom::cells` and `MeshedModel::from_exchange` into `Physics::solve(&Mesh, ...)`, with canonical sorted edges, $D_3$ triangle-face permutations (`0..6`), $D_4$ dihedral quad-face symmetries (`0..8`), curved `edge_nodes` / `quad_face_nodes`, `qp_edges`, and `validate_conforming` enforcing **zero transition pyramids** (`Tet` $\leftrightarrow$ `Prism` triangle bases and `Prism` $\leftrightarrow$ `Hex` quad walls match node-for-node).
  * `fem_mixed` provides separable tensor-product quadrature, $C^0$-conforming `MixedLagrangeDofMap` ($Q_1$/$P_1\otimes Q_1$/$Q_1$ at order 1; serendipity 8-node `Quad`, 15-node `Prism`, 20-node `Hex` for `nyx-sms` and `nyx-dfs` at order 2; full 9/18/27-node $Q_2$ tensor spaces for exact de Rham sequences), and isoparametric `CurvedQuad`, `CurvedPrism`, and `CurvedHex` mappings.
  * `edge_mixed` (`NedMixed`) provides trace-compatible Nédélec $H(\mathrm{curl})$ elements ($N_1$: $4/9/12$ DOFs; $N_2$: $12/36/54$ DOFs on `Quad`/`Prism`/`Hex` matching `Tet` `Ned3` bit-for-bit across shared faces via covariant Piola transforms) and exact discrete `gradient_p1_full`, `gradient_p2_full`, and `p1_to_p2_full` operators consumed directly by unmodified `Ams` and `AmsP2` (`femcore::linalg::ams`).
  * Pure-simplex `SimplexMesh` fast paths stay untouched when `mesh.is_pure_simplex()`, `SimplexMesh::from_cells_split_with` retains positive 3D determinant orientation and `edge_nodes`/`qp_edges` for fallback callers, and Stage-3 `MixedToSimplexProjector` projects nodal and per-cell fields onto `SplitMode::Minimal` `SimplexMesh` lazily so `h5io`, `xdmf`, `view3d`, and `reduce` require zero format changes.
* **Outward boundary-extruded absorbing layers, all 7 canonical topologies, lockstep curved shells & ray-invariance guard (amending ADR 69, extending ADR 52 & ADR 82; `geom`, `femcore::meshing::{pml_box,pml_cyl,pml_sphere}`):**
  * `geom::Op::Pml` (`add_absorbing_layer` / `add_pml`) gains serde-defaulted `mode` (`Inward` default for legacy JSON so all 26 `geom` goldens round-trip byte-for-byte; `Outward` default for new workflows), optional `window` (`None` extrudes directly from $\partial\Omega_{\text{phys}}$ with zero CAD padding), `coord` (`Auto | Cartesian | Cylindrical | Spherical`), `center`, `axis`, and `layer_grading` (decoupled from stretch exponent `order`), plus `"auto"` sentinels on `thickness` and `layers`. `geom::stack` lifts the standalone curved-host refusal for 3D `Sphere` outer hosts.
  * `pml_box`, `pml_cyl`, and `pml_sphere` sweep uncut `Quad` (2D) and `Prism` + `Hex` (3D) layers across all **7 canonical topologies**: `2D Cartesian` (4 faces + 4 corners), `2D-axi Cartesian` (3 faces + 2 outer corners, auto-omitting `XMin` at $r = 0$), `2D Cylindrical` (1 concentric curved `Quad` `RadialShell`), `2D-axi Spherical` (1 concentric curved `Quad` `SphericalShell` clamping $\vartheta = 0, \pi$ poles to exact IEEE `r = 0.0` and preserving `r = 0` symmetry facets), `3D Cartesian` (6 `Prism` faces + 12 `Hex` edges + 8 `Hex` corners = 26 sub-domains), `3D Cylindrical` (barrel `Prism` + 2 axial endcap `Prism`s + 2 `CornerAnnulus` `Hex` rings), and `3D Spherical` (concentric `Prism` shells with zero pyramids). Extruded columns inherit base-facet materials ($\partial_u\varepsilon \equiv 0$), extrude interior `Op::Sheet` boundary edges into lateral/radial conductive `Quad` sheet facets, and project concentric `edge_nodes`/`quad_face_nodes` in lockstep across shells $R_0 \dots R_{N_L}$ (preventing thin-layer sagitta inversion while interior `curve3`/`sheet3d_curve` skip PML cells).
  * `Evaluated::validate_pml_ray_invariance` rejects physically illegal `Spherical` PML across multi-layer horizontal wafers or off-center interfaces ($\partial_R\varepsilon \neq 0$) and rejects `Cylindrical` PML when waveguides or `Op::Sheet` traces pierce $r = R_{\text{in}}$ ($\partial_r\varepsilon \neq 0$).
* **Physics-controlled defaults, quadrature-point curvilinear pullbacks, dual profiles & dimensionless auto-scaling (`femcore::{props,pml}`, `nyx-ems`, `nyx-engine`, `app`):**
  * `MeshRequest` and `MeshSettings` default to `pml_mode: PmlModeRequest::Auto` (deserializing legacy `structured_pml: Option<bool>` cleanly), skip wasted 2D inline CDT/Ruppert PML triangulation, wire structured 3D PML extrusion into `sweep_model_3d`, disable `pml_relax = 1.5` coarsening inside structured PMLs (`pml_relax = 1.0`), scale `Op::Pml::layers` inside `convergence::refined`, and report `element_counts`, `min_pml_scaled_jacobian`, `< 5` layer `pml_warnings`, and `pml_diagnostics` (`s_dist_bounds` $\in [0, 1]$).
  * `femcore::pml::pml_jacobian` and `pullback_maxwell` evaluate coordinate-free complex-symmetric Ward–Pendry tensors at each Gauss quadrature point $\mathbf{x}_q$ across `Cartesian`, `Cylindrical`, and `Spherical` coordinates (regular as $r \to 0^+$ with $s_\theta = \tilde{R}(R)/R$). `femcore::pml::auto_scale_params` and `nyx-engine::pml::add_pml_conditions` calibrate coupled multi-material groups jointly to $(\lambda_{\min}, \lambda_{\max})$ and target reflection $R_0 = 10^{-6}$ so $\tilde{\mathbf{x}}$ remains $C^0$-continuous across shared interfaces, selecting bounded $\omega$-independent `KappaPolynomial` ABCS stretching for eigenfrequency / iterative `AMS` solves and locking-free `Rational` stretching for direct-solved open problems.
  * `nyx-ems` widens `mu_inv` to symmetric $3\times 3$ `[[C; 3]; 3]`, reformulates `axisym` covariantly via $\Psi = i r E_\theta$ to allow meshes touching $r = 0$ with exact $m$-dependent axis regularity ($m = 0$: $\Psi = 0$; $|m| = 1$: $E_z = \Psi = 0$; $|m| \ge 2$: both $0$), safeguards iterative `AMS`/`AmsP2` by falling back from `Rational` to `KappaPolynomial`, partitions broadband `driven` PROM sweeps ($f_{\max}/f_{\min} > 3$) into octave sub-bands sharing one symbolic factorization, and reports radiated power `P_pml (W)`. In `app` (`ADR 123`, `ADR 124`), `"pml"` and `"infinite_element"` domains share the quiet grey hatched `"PML"` role, the Mesh tab exposes the 3-way `Auto | Structured | Inline` selector, and the Physics tab adds one-click **"Add PML Conditions from Geometry"**.
* **Elastodynamic backward-wave protection, signed-$Q$ honesty, outer clamping & static IE (`nyx-sms`, `femcore::meshing::physics_size`):**
  * `backward_wave_mpml_ratio` evaluates exact Hellmann–Feynman group velocities on a 400-point folded Fibonacci sphere + near-grazing rings to detect antiparallel slowness concavities ($k_u v_{g,u} < 0$) and recommend $p_{\min}(\hat{\mathbf{u}})$. `nyx-sms` supports `stabilization = "auto" | "off" | "mpml" | "adiabatic"` (`"auto"` activating M-PML tangential damping when $p_{\min} > 0$; `"adiabatic"` multiplying $\mathbf{C}$ by $1 + i\eta_{\max}t^2$).
  * `nyx-sms` removes `f.im.abs()` sign masking, reporting signed `Q = Re f / (2 Im f)` and flagging `f.im < -1e-9 * f.re` modes with `[AMPLIFYING backward-wave mode]`, `unstable_modes`, and solver warnings alongside `P_pml N (W)`. Exterior PML termination walls default to Dirichlet clamping (`clamp_outer = true`), eliminating spurious rigid-body modes, and `sms::static` gains native mixed-cell `Rational` and `Exponential` `"infinite_element"` domains (rejected on dynamic studies).
* **3-tier open-boundary hierarchy for diffusion and statics (extending ADR 115 & ADR 120; `nyx-dfs`):**
  * **Tier 1:** 2D layered-wafer `planar_bem` (`ADR 115`) remains unchanged for zero-thickness planar conductors.
  * **Tier 2 (`far_field` Robin):** Extends `"far_field"` (`Applies::Boundary`) to `heat_stationary` (conductivity-scaled Order-1/Order-2 Robin around `t_inf`, default `293.15 K`) and `magnetostatic` (3D 1st-order asymptotic curl-Robin $\int_\Gamma \frac{\gamma}{\mu_0\mu_r r}(\hat{\mathbf{n}}\times\mathbf{A}_a)\cdot(\hat{\mathbf{n}}\times\mathbf{A}_b)\,dS$ on `Tri3`/`Quad4` facets and 2D logarithmic Robin).
  * **Tier 3 (`infinite_element` & `heat_transient` guard):** Registers `"infinite_element"` (`Applies::Domain`, `Rational` $[R_{\text{in}}, R_{\text{out}}) \to [R_{\text{in}}, \infty)$ and `Exponential` + automatic outer Order-1 `far_field` Robin) on `electrostatic`, `heat_stationary`, and `magnetostatic` over native `Quad`/`Prism`/`Hex` layers using element-interpolated radial coordinates for locking-free $P_1$/$P_2$ multipole decay. Explicitly rejects `"infinite_element"` and `"far_field"` on parabolic `heat_transient` with guidance to use unstretched ($s_u \equiv 1$) diffusion-length shells $d = \sqrt{4 D_{\max} t_{\text{end}}\ln(1/R_0)}$.

## Consequences

* Native `Quad`/`Prism`/`Hex` assembly cuts PML element counts by $2\times\text{–}6\times$, reduces 3D PML Nédélec/Lagrange DOFs by $29\%\text{–}57\%$, accelerates direct `LBL^H` factorization, preserves exact discrete de Rham cohomology ($\|K G_{1,2}\|_\infty < 10^{-13}$) and $C_{4v}$ doublet symmetry ($|\Delta f / f| < 10^{-13}$), and eliminates tangential locking under singular Rational PML and Infinite Element poles ($< 0.03\%$ $P_1$ and $< 0.001\%$ $P_2$ sphere capacitance error at $R_{\text{in}} = 2R$; $> 80\times$ magnetostatic loop-inductance box-bias reduction).
* New projects define only $\Omega_{\text{phys}}$ and attach an outward absorbing layer without manual CAD padding or dummy `window` primitives, while all existing `.nyx.json` projects and numerical/geometry golden files continue to parse and pass unchanged.
