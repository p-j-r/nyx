# Rough spots: inconsistencies, duplication and coupling that the model shows

Found while building `workspace.dsl` (2026-11-03, against main `a047506`). Each item says what it is, where, why it matters, and what would fix it.
Nothing here was changed in the code; items that need a decision from paul are marked **[decide]**. Severity: **H** will bite soon, **M** costs time, **L** tidiness.

Related: ADR numbers refer to `adr/`, contract names to `contracts/`.

## What is in good shape (so the list is not only bad news)

* `section.rs` is a real single point for axis numbers, and the wafer-frame tests (`frame.rs`, `frame_roundtrip.rs`, `wafer_names.rs`, `ring_frame.rs`) exercise it from three sides.
* Kernel stages (`stages.rs`) already prove that `solve_*` equals assemble, solve, fields for each kernel.
* `geom` has golden models with closed-form expectations and bit-exact serde round-trip tests.
* Determinism has a test (`determinism.rs`, `solvers/determinism`).
* Every library file begins with a header comment; the history in git is one commit per decision with a descriptive subject, which is what the ADRs were written from.

## Resolved by the restructure (mesh phase, 2026-11-04; details in ADR 31 and 32)

* **R01** two `CellMesh` and two `SimplexMesh` types: `mesh::cells::CellMesh<D>` is the one mesh; the nyx `CellMesh`/`ElementType` are deleted; `mesh::SimplexMesh` is the solver view (to be removed in phase 4); the adaptation's private working mesh is `adapt::work::SimplexMesh<D>`.
* **R03** (mesher part): `meshers/par.rs` is gone; its fixed-chunk reductions live in `femcore::par` (ADR 11 amendment).
* **R04** three PML mechanisms: `pml_box::build_pml` is the one generator of structured layers; `pml_geom` and `pml3` are adapters; the inline layers are a property of the geom model.
* **R05** legacy 3D mesher: `mesher3d`/`refine3d` left the library and are kept as the test fixture `crates/femcore/tests/common/solid3d` (paul wants them kept; a geom adapter for general 3D shapes is a separate thread).
* **R09** label bases: domains and boundaries are numbered from 1 everywhere, no shift code left; contract `mesh_cells.v1.json`.

## Resolved by the restructure (analysis phase, 2026-11-05; details in ADR 56)

* **R28** the polarization analysis named its axes differently from the wafer frame: it is in `nyx-analysis` now, its samples are `lat`, `nor`, `prop` (what the component is), the documentation maps them to the wafer axes x, z and y, and a result states its frame (`field frame`). The labels of the Analysis tab did not change (they were wafer names already).

## Resolved at the close-out (phase 9, 2026-11-13; ADR 63, 64, 65)

* **R06** two expression evaluators: kept as two copies (geom must not depend on femcore), and made safe to keep: `crates/femcore/tests/expr_grammar.rs` runs one corpus through both, they accept and refuse the same inputs and agree on every value to the last bit.
* **R08** three hash functions: the identity rule is written down (ADR 64): `nyx_store::util::hash_bytes` (BLAKE3) is the only persisted hash; every other hash is a cache key and never leaves the process. `nyx-session` and its FNV handles no longer exist.
* **R14** docs that record states code has moved past: left as records on purpose (ADR 65); `docs/architecture/` and `CLAUDE.md` are the place for what is true now. `docs/CAPABILITIES_MEMO.md` was never added (R24); `FEATURE_DEPENDENCIES.md` is in `docs/ports/meshers/`.
* **R18** `geom` dependency direction: `crates/nyx-engine/tests/layering.rs` has the crate table (geom depends on no workspace crate) and `geom_depends_on_serde_and_optional_rayon_only`.
* **R20** no version marker on saved interfaces: the `format` key in `Project`, `FeatureList` and `ProblemSpec` (ADR 63).
* **R23** lints and formatting: `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is clean; `rustfmt` stays on the crates that already follow it (ADR 65); `rust-version` is 1.99.
* **R13** stale names: comments fixed (`pml3.rs`, `mesh3d.rs`, `physics_size.rs`, the front-end texts). The public stage functions of the cavity solver keep `emw` in their names (`assemble_emw_eigen`, `solve_emw_modes`, `emw_eigen_fields`, `EmwAssembly`, `nyx-ems/src/cavity.rs`): they are pinned by the `kernel_stages` contract and the golden tests, so renaming them is a contract change nobody asked for. The test files `emw2d.rs` and `emw3d.rs` of `nyx-ems` keep their names for the same reason (goldens refer to them).

* **R32** curved geometry (ADR 82) is used by the `ems` cavity study only: `sms` and `dfs` see straight cells in 3D, and the plotted quadratic nodes and the XDMF cells stay at the straight positions. Neither logs a warning when the mesh has curved edges yet.
* **R33** creases between two curved faces, and a curved face that meets a periodic face, keep straight edges (`Domain3::edge_on_crease`; `project_edge` of a periodic pair is not matched). `capability-r7-periodic-cells` must give matching curved edges on paired faces (call `curve_boundary_edges` after its face copy).
* **R34** curved cells inside an absorbing layer are allowed (the stretch is per cell) but untested; the integrals of `nyx-analysis` and `nyx-ems` that are not the matrices (energy, mode volume, layer fraction of the cavity study) use straight measures.
* **R35** the lattice mesher has no chord limit unless `max_dev` or a face size is set (ADR 83); the congruence of refined periodic faces is not solved (a face size that reaches a periodic face is refused). `set_mesh` rejected `max_dev`, `structured_pml` and `points_per_wavelength` before ADR 83.

Resolved earlier, by the phase named in the plan and recorded in `docs/RESTRUCTURE_STATUS.md` and the ADRs of that phase: R02, R03, R26 (phase 3, ADR 33), R10, R11, R12, R19 (the explicit `Ctx` for the physics; the thread-local progress sink remains inside femcore and is installed per job by the engine), R27 (phase 4, ADR 25, 41 to 47), R07, R15, R16, R17, R21, R25 (phases 4 and 7, ADR 26, 28, 57, 59), R22 (gate G2: tolerances in `nyx-ems/tests/golden`, no reference platform), R24 (phase 0).
